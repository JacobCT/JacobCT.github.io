## International gravitational-wave analysis

The International Pulsar Timing Array brings together data, expertise, and analysis methods from pulsar timing collaborations around the world. Combining these resources provides a powerful basis for studying nanohertz gravitational waves and for testing results across independent data sets and analysis approaches.

## Contributing to IPTA DR3

I actively participate in the IPTA Gravitational Wave Analysis Working Group and follow the development of the collaboration's analysis programme. I will contribute to the gravitational-wave analysis of IPTA DR3, working with the wider collaboration to help validate and interpret the results from this international data set.
