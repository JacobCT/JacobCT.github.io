## Searching across the sky

Continuous waves from a slowly evolving binary should produce a correlated signal across the pulsars in a timing array. We searched over sky position and gravitational-wave frequency, using the <em>F</em><sub>p</sub> and <em>F</em><sub>e</sub> statistics alongside Bayesian analyses.

No significant signal was found. The resulting strain upper limits reach approximately 7 × 10<sup>−15</sup> near 10 nHz and improve on the previous PPTA search by at least a factor of four across 1–200 nHz. For binaries with chirp masses above 10<sup>9</sup> M<sub>☉</sub>, the search probes distances of roughly 50–200 Mpc over its most sensitive frequency range.
