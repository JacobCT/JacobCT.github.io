## Connecting gravitational-wave frequency bands

Pulsar timing arrays and LISA probe different parts of the gravitational-wave spectrum, but they address closely related questions about how massive black holes form, grow, and merge. I am particularly interested in how the methods developed for targeted PTA searches—including electromagnetic source selection, multimessenger parameter constraints, and careful source modelling—can inform complementary work within the LISA community.

## Looking for shared opportunities

I follow developments in the LISA mission closely and look for opportunities where the two communities can work together. Potential connections include consistent modelling of massive-black-hole populations, coordinated use of electromagnetic observations, transferable statistical methods, and joint efforts to understand how sources evolve across the gravitational-wave spectrum. My aim is to help develop these links as PTA and LISA science continue to mature.
