## Mapping neutral hydrogen in M33

The data were used to investigate the motion of M33 and the structure of its neutral-hydrogen emission. From the observed velocity pattern, we inferred that the galaxy rotates counter-clockwise relative to the observing position while its systemic motion is toward the observer.

The analysis also produced an estimated mass of approximately 10<sup>11</sup> solar masses. Moment maps were used to interpret the spatial and velocity structure of the three-dimensional data cube.

## Additional observational studies

### X-ray analysis of the Vela Pulsar

Archival Chandra observations were separated into soft- and hard-X-ray bands to compare their spectra and spatial distributions and to investigate the physical mechanisms behind the emission. [Explore the Vela Pulsar analysis &rarr;]({{ '/projects/vela-pulsar-xray-analysis/' | relative_url }})

### Exploring the 2MASS catalogue

Infrared point-source measurements were used to identify a population separated from the main stellar sequence in a colour-colour diagram and evaluate its possible physical interpretation. [Explore the 2MASS catalogue analysis &rarr;]({{ '/projects/2mass-astronomical-catalogue/' | relative_url }})

### Imaging the Orion Nebula

Visible-light observations from the White Hall Telescope were calibrated to reduce instrumental noise and compare wavelength-dependent structure in the nebula. [Explore the Orion imaging project &rarr;]({{ '/projects/orion-nebula-astrophotography/' | relative_url }})
